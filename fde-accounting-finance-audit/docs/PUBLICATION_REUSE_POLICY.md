# Publication and rights policy

**This public page distributes original bibliographic metadata, original scientific synthesis, and original engineering test plans.** It does not republish third-party dissertations, journal PDFs, publisher figures, datasets or paid newsletter text.

- DOI titles/authors are bibliographic facts with links to publisher records.
- Thesis entries link back to institutional repositories and clearly state their academic status.
- TLDR articles are attributed to the source and mapped to proposed engineering controls; TLDR is a news discovery channel, not a journal indexing authority or evidence of model performance.
- Bibliographic inclusion does not imply endorsement by a university, publisher, ranking provider, or technology company.
- The demo's Apache-2.0 license covers the team's original public software/text only; it does not extend to linked third-party works.
- No protected financial records, private working papers, credentials or unpublished manuscripts are copied into the public tree.
- Any later replication code must be checked for copyright/license terms and exact study alignment.
- Any Q1 claim explicitly refers to the 2025 SCImago/Scopus quartile; AJG 4/4* refers to the 2024 guide; FT50 to 2026 membership.

**Evidence priority:** applicable professional standards and independently verified source documents → peer-reviewed, methodologically relevant studies → institutional theses (supporting methods) → public preprints with clear status → TLDR news discovery (technology horizon scan only).

The software itself has no validated financial statement audit outcomes or production deployment and must not be marketed as certified or profession-approved.
