**Literature-derived design propositions, not results:** (1) human oversight must be substantive, not ceremonial; (2) evidence-gated drafting may reduce unsupported AI claims, but this must be tested; (3) optimization must use independently adjudicated accounting outcomes; (4) efficient routine automation may still conceal extreme-tail errors; (5) audit exceptions must connect to account assertions, verified evidence and control owners.

**Existing software evidence:** The API includes explicit rules and synthetic checks. **Not yet implemented:** a professional reviewer study, validated audit outcome dataset, evidence verification, controlled human–AI experiment or measured business impact.
