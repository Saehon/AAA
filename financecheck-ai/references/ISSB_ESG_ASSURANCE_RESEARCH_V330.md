# NAAIL Research & Assurance V3.3 — Authority, Scope and Reviewer Gates

Snapshot **2026-11-14**. Date-controlled (not permanently current). This product is an independent educational/research tool. **No IFRS Foundation, ISSB, IAASB, PCAOB, EFRAG, FT or XBRL certification/endorsement.**

## Five evidence tracks
1. **ISSB IFRS S1/S2 investor-oriented financial materiality**. Do not treat financial materiality as EU ESRS double materiality. The 2025 IFRS S2 greenhouse-gas amendments are effective from 2027-01-01 unless early applied.
2. **ESRS / EU double materiality**: impacts (inside-out) and financial risks/opportunities (outside-in), with affected people/environment, value chains, reporting year and period-specific regime. For 2026, revised ESRS transition options are subject to the EU Commission 2026/1563 rules; version recorded in sustainability statement. 2027 revised baseline.
3. **IAASB sustainability assurance**: ISAE 3410 historical GHG route, ISSA 5000 internationally effective for reporting periods beginning on/after 2026-12-15 (or as-at that date), effective national adoption must be checked independently; ISAE 3000 (Revised) belongs in historical/non-sustainability contexts as applicable. Local amendments may differ.
4. **PCAOB audit and ICFR**: AS 1105, AS 1215, AS 2201, AS 3101; financial audit evidence, CAM and auditor documentation. Not a sustainability assurance standard. Check date-sensitive changes effective Dec 15, 2026.
5. **FT50 and AJG4 research**: FT50 2026 = *50 journal-name entries* sourced from University of Alberta; AJG 2024 4/4* = only a verified **subset** for accounting/finance, NOT all AJG grade-4 journals. Academic studies must be individually located, read, DOI-verified, replication-checked and judged. No downloaded paper corpus, no FT50/ABS4 copyright reuse.

## Golden output contract
Each finding needs: question; jurisdiction; reporting-period begin/end; governing framework and version; original URL and actual inspected passages if any; extracted numbers with units/definition; source-authentication grade; interpretation and alternative hypotheses; uncertainty; reviewer action/status.

Statuses: NOT_TESTED, SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE, CALCULATED_FROM_SUPPLIED_INPUTS, INSPECTED_PRIMARY_SOURCE, CONFLICTED_EVIDENCE, HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Never confuse claims in a prompt with inspected evidence. Never invent audit assurance, legal conformity, a paper DOI, or a regulatory certification.

## Important licence and ethics controls
Do not bundle copyrighted full IFRS/ISSB/IAASB/ESRS standards text beyond clearly permissible brief paraphrases; consult rights-holder licensing before embedding taxonomy packages, complete standards, proprietary datasets or commercial reports. No login/account data, personal/private client information or vendor-licensed files in public code.
