# FinanceCheck Discover — user comprehension and usability protocol

**Status: PROTOCOL ONLY. No participants enrolled, no findings, no analytics installed.**

## Decision
Does a transparent 3-card financial explanation increase correct understanding without obscuring source status compared with an equally accurate concise text answer?

## Preliminary usability pilot
- Suggested **12 consenting adults** across beginner and experienced reader groups, with accessible invitation and voluntary withdrawal. This is a usability/pretest sample only, not a statistically powered efficacy study.
- Use fictional financial questions (profit vs cash flow, revenue growth, depreciation). Avoid collecting real bank balances, investor holdings, income or any unnecessary personal information.
- Randomize which version is first (A: concise text; B: three source-labeled cards) and counterbalance question order to limit learning effects. No dark-patterns, TikTok-style infinite scroll or engagement pressure.
- After exposure ask 2–3 objective comprehension questions and one evidence-status question (e.g., "Were the figures independently verified?"). Measure correct answer, time to accurate answer (with opt-in consent), completion and perceived cognitive effort.
- Record only aggregated consented data; define deletion/retention and GDPR lawful basis before collecting anything. Use consent where legally appropriate; institutional ethics review as required.
- Count any *false impression that a fictional number was source-verified* as a severe product safety finding.
- Report observed counts and uncertainty, with no claims of statistical significance from 12 participants.

## Larger preregistered study
Only after pilot repairs, power a new independent experiment using prespecified minimally important comprehension effect and baseline variance. Do not fabricate sample size targets or declare statistical superiority without genuine data.

## Consumer success metric
The principal goal is correct understanding of a public-finance question **and** correct calibration of source certainty. Voluntary returns and shares are secondary; long screen time, intrusive notifications and compulsive engagement are not goals.
