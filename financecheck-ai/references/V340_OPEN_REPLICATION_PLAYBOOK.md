# Free research/code workflow (technical research preview)

1. Query OpenAlex/Crossref for bibliographic identity and cross-check publisher DOI.
2. Independently confirm journal name against *dated* FT50/AJG registers; SSRN and theses are working/dissertation materials, not FT50 published articles.
3. Find repositories and dataset landing pages; check item-level license, metadata, version, maintainer, checksum/commit, access terms, provenance and privacy.
4. Separate public SEC EDGAR filing records from other licensed feeds or unverified sample mergers.
5. Reproduce published outcome in isolated environment only after malicious-code review, rights review and explicit execution. Pin seed, versions and run commands. Report success/failure objectively.
6. For scientific discovery: generate, reflect/falsify, rank, evolve, reproduce and meta-review; human reviewer signs off. The V3.4 local pipeline creates tasks only, it does not call actual Google Co-Scientist or AlphaEvolve.
7. University repositories are ten curated examples, *not* a current published ranking. Restriction/embargo per thesis overrides repository-level open access.
