# FinanceCheck Discover: social-inspired, finance-safe UX

**Audience:** people reading annual reports for the first time, students, small-business readers and finance-interested learners. Not a stock picker.

### The first screen
Hook ≤14 words, truthful and non-alarmist. Answer visible in ≤70 words. Always mark fictional examples. Use a concept-specific chart only when accurate inputs exist. Three optional actions: Read source, Show calculation, Learn concept. No forced account creation.

### Story template
Card 1: claim + correct value + fiscal period + currency + evidence state.
Card 2: explanation (comparison basis and arithmetic); one meaningful counterpoint.
Card 3: source permalink + what remains uncertain + optional next question. Keep captions accessible, no small-screen footnotes hiding uncertainty.

### Consent and user autonomy
Opt-in personalization (beginner/intermediate/professional, topics) only; no sensitive profiling or dark patterns. No endless feed, push notifications, streak-pressure, simulated human investment advice or social-risk gamification.

### Growth experiment
A/B: concise vs expanded default on beginner cohort; 3-card optional view vs text with same verified inputs. Primary = verified task completion / eligible users. Guardrails = source hallucination incidents, privacy breaches, comprehension errors, perceived manipulation. Freeze assignment, data retention, denominator, confidence method and minimum clinically/educationally relevant uplift before rollout. Never treat retention gains as evidence of better financial accuracy.
