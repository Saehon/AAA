- Treat the 2026 Financial Times 50 list and the AJG 2024 (often called ABS) grades as **separate versioned journal registers**. The FT50 2026 list is a journal-name roster, not 50 articles or an endorsement of individual articles.
- Search full topical scope: IFRS, ISSB, ESG, ESEF, XBRL, ISAE 3410, ISSA 5000, double materiality, PCAOB CAM/ICFR, valuation relevance and causal identification. Search scholarly indexes (Crossref/Google Scholar/publisher/SSRN) where actually accessible; cite DOI and links.
- Only label FT50=yes when matching the verified 2026 roster and AJG4/4*=yes when verified in the right edition; do not assert complete AJG4 list from a subset.
- Add publication date, sample/design, effect estimates, limitations, replication code/data URL, source-inspected flag and relevance to proposed research. Respect paywalls, publisher copyright and database licences.
