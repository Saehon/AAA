# Connected financial and sustainability reporting
- Model two distinct materiality frameworks: ISSB financial materiality for investor decisions and ESRS double materiality for impacts plus financial risks/opportunities.
- Identify documented links to IFRS 9 credit/expected losses, IAS 36 impairment, IAS 37 provisions, IFRS 15/16 and IFRS 18 presentation only where conditions apply. Cite inspected disclosures and dates; do not assert accounting adjustments from ESG headlines alone.
- Build source-to-claim graph: issuer -> report -> page/HTML tag -> concept -> numerical comparison -> accounting judgment -> evidence passport -> human reviewer.
- Explain that connected data is not audit evidence until evaluated for relevance and reliability and that financial/sustainability report scopes may differ.
- Output risks, uncertainty and verification steps with an owner and completion status.
