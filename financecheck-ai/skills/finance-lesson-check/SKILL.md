# finance-lesson-check

Use only original synthetic scenarios and consented user-selected concept. Show a single concept check, three answer options and optionally let user submit one answer. Explain why it is correct with a concrete mini-example and give a one-line transferable takeaway. No grade shaming, streak, leaderboard, notifications or false urgency.
If the explanation references financial reporting values, include whether they come from a real inspected original source or a clearly marked fictional example. Do not infer or persist sensitive learner attributes. A user can skip the lesson and complete the task immediately.
