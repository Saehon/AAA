# Sustainability and audit empirical research
- Specify a falsifiable research question (not merely a technological proposal), conceptual framework, variables with observable public/licensed data origins, research sample, identification strategy and replication code plan.
- Possible candidates: (i) ESEF tagging quality and materiality disclosure comparability; (ii) ISSB climate transparency and financing costs; (iii) assurance regime transition and GHG credibility; (iv) climate exposure and ICFR/CAM evidence risk.
- Distinguish actual source access from planned data: SEC EDGAR, EFRAG taxonomy, ESMA ESEF publications, issuer sustainability reports, Refinitiv/ASSET4 or Audit Analytics *only when licensed/connected*.
- Support hypotheses with individually verified FT50 and AJG4 studies; never fake DOI, estimates, sample size or replication links. Perform adversarial Reviewer #2 critique and pre-registration checklist.
