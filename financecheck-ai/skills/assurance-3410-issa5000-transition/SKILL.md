# ISAE 3410 → ISSA 5000 transition
- Request jurisdiction, engagement subject matter, assurance period start/end, reporting-specific date, assurance standard named in report and assurance level.
- As of 2026-11-14, IAASB had approved withdrawal of ISAE 3410 effective 2026-12-15; ISSA 5000 is internationally effective for information periods beginning on or after 2026-12-15, or as at specific date on or after that date. **Jurisdictional adoption and later local deadlines vary.**
- Treat ISAE 3410 as historical/transitional GHG context; ISAE 3000 (Revised) as historical/non-sustainability context where applicable. Do not recommend it as a default current sustainability assurance framework after jurisdictional ISSA 5000 implementation.
- Distinguish reasonable versus limited assurance, underlying criteria, subject matter, risk assessment, evidence sufficiency, competence, ethics and reporting conclusion.
- Produce a dated applicability note plus questions for the engagement partner. Never claim to perform professional assurance.
