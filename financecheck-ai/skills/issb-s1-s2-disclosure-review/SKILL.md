# ISSB S1/S2 disclosure review
1. Request issuer, reporting period, jurisdiction, disclosed adoption basis and *original* public sustainability statement; do not assume global mandatory applicability.
2. Evaluate IFRS S1 risk/opportunity linkage to prospects using the ISSB four-part structure: governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets. Evaluate climate-related areas under IFRS S2, including scope 1/2/3 disclosures, methodology, estimates and industry metrics where relevant.
3. Distinguish ISSB investor-oriented financial materiality from ESRS impact + financial double materiality. Do not infer equivalence.
4. Note December 2025 IFRS S2 targeted GHG amendments effective for reporting periods beginning 2027-01-01, with early application permitted. Verify applicable rules and transition relief.
5. Produce a claim/evidence matrix with report URL, period, disclosure topic, exact page/tag when inspected, evidence quality, reviewer query and missing evidence. Never fabricate a page number.
6. This is a research checklist, not a complete standards-conformity review, IFRS certification or assurance opinion.
Sources: IFRS Foundation IFRS S1/S2 Knowledge Hub and 2025 IFRS S2 amendments.
