# Educational accounting close
Run public-data-privacy-gate. Accept only public or synthetic material. No real ledgers, payroll, personal bank accounts, client audit records or ERP connections. If a deterministic checker is available, test debit-credit totals, exact reference+amount+currency one-to-one matches, gross-profit and operating-income ties, and budget-to-actual driver bridges. Do not authenticate user-provided source numbers or causally attribute variances without evidence.
For SOX, prepare only a **draft workpaper template** (risk, assertion, control, period, sample, evidence, exception, reviewer); never approve a control or certify compliance. Preserve source dates, reporting basis, currency, limitations, unresolved issues and reviewer handoff.
