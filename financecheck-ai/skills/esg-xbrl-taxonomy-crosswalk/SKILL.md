# ESG taxonomy and XBRL evidence crosswalk
- Record taxonomy owner, licensed package version, namespace, reporting period, entity, period type, context, unit, dimensional axis, extension, anchoring and citation to original taxonomy definitions.
- Keep ISSB Sustainability Disclosure Taxonomy, IFRS Accounting Taxonomy, European ESRS taxonomy and SEC US-GAAP XBRL namespaces distinct; never invent 1:1 equivalences.
- Build human-reviewed semantic mappings and mark them EQUIVALENT / PARTIAL / INCOMPATIBLE / NOT_REVIEWED. Do not manufacture tags or assert Arelle/ESEF conformance without running authorised validator against the exact taxonomies.
- ESG narrative claims require source passages and financial statement links with report-date and materiality caveats.
