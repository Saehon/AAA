# FT50/AJG4 reproducibility reviewer
- Inspect paper and author-supplied supplements. Record whether actual replication scripts, dictionaries and data are accessible, whether they are legally reusable, and what commands execute.
- Review units of observation, sample, period, identifiers, joins, treatment assignment, selection, missingness, clustering, parallel trends, placebo, sensitivity and alternative explanations. Separate association from causal inference.
- Define three outputs: replicated as reported, partially reproduced, or NOT_TESTED. Never attribute a result to a paper unless actually examined.
- For agentic augmentation, propose baseline econometrics vs agent-generated results with blinded human-reviewed gold and false-positive rate; avoid claiming publication potential or novelty without search and reviewer validation.
