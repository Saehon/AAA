# GHG data provenance and quality review
- Require emissions inventory period, organizational boundary, measurement method, gas/GWP basis, Scope 1/2/3 category, CO2e unit, market/location electricity method and original source.
- Separate direct activity data, conversion factors, estimates, supplier information and auditor assurance status. Do not normalize emissions unless scopes and methods are comparable.
- Identify revised reporting methods, base-year restatements, scope 3 category 15 financed emissions limitations and December 2025 IFRS S2 targeted amendments (effective 2027 unless early applied).
- Flag missing emission factor sources, counterfactual baselines, unit scaling and boundary changes; report uncertainties and evidence status.
- Mark outcomes DESCRIBED, CALCULATED_FROM_INPUTS, INSPECTED_SOURCE, CONTRADICTED, REQUIRES_REVIEW. No assurance opinion.
