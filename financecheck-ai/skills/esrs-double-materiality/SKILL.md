# ESRS double materiality
- Apply the relevant ESRS version for the jurisdiction and fiscal-year commencement; EU Delegated Regulation 2026/1563 allows transitional choices for 2026 and applies from 2027.
- Separately analyze **impact materiality** (people/environment, scale, scope, remediability, actual/potential impacts and likelihood where relevant) and **financial materiality** (cash flows, access to finance, cost of capital, risks and opportunities over specified horizons).
- Document activities, value chain, stakeholder input, quantitative/qualitative thresholds and methodology. Record whether each threshold has management approval.
- Never use an invented universal score or equate an automatically estimated numeric score with an ESRS materiality conclusion. Produce candidates and missing-information flags only; independent professional adjudication is required.
- For each IRO include direction (inside-out vs outside-in), time horizon, source/evidence, version of ESRS used, uncertainties, responsible reviewer and pending decisions.
