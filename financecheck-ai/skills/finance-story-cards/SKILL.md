# finance-story-cards

Only use public/synthetic inputs allowed by public-data-privacy-gate. For a real company, obtain and inspect authentic original figures before asserting results; never infer credibility from a supplied URL alone.
Return a 3-card story: (1) important fact with entity+period, (2) plain-language why/how with exact calculation, (3) source + what remains unknown. Optional 20–30 second spoken script, caption and 3-5 relevant tags; no platform logos, impersonation, trading tips, claims of guaranteed returns or unlicensed music.
For every *published* financial card, preserve the company, accounting basis, currency/unit, fiscal period, source status, and original-document citation in the card or accompanying caption. If no source was inspected, make the label "Submitted numbers — not source verified" visibly prominent and never invent a source URL or a verification badge. For fictional examples show "Fictional educational example". Keep one useful fact per card rather than dense tables.
Choose user-controlled 'save or share' only. Do not implement infinite scroll, dark patterns or nagging notifications. Check with the independent reviewer-engineer skill before marking publishable.

**Quality criteria:** The first card is intelligible in one glance; the second shows a reproducible calculation or an explicit not-executed label; the third gives source and caveat. Do not claim a rendered image or share action unless a supported tool actually produced it.
