# PCAOB audit evidence, CAM and ICFR research
- Scope explicitly to US issuer financial statements/ICFR; PCAOB standards do NOT independently govern an ISSB or ESRS sustainability assurance engagement merely because the issuer is US-listed.
- Map source evidence to AS 1105 (audit evidence), AS 1215 (documentation), AS 2201 (ICFR integrated audit), AS 3101 (critical audit matters), AS 2110 (risk) and AS 2301 (response). Verify effective text for engagement date, especially Dec 15 2026 amendments.
- Distinguish CAM from KAM, risk indicators from actual deficiencies, documentation from auditor sign-off. 
- For climate-related financial assumptions, map to asset impairment, useful life, provisions, fair value and disclosed estimates only where financial statement connections exist.
- Create review questions and evidence passports with record-level provenance. No actual PCAOB inspection opinion or audit assurance.
