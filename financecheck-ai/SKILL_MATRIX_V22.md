# FinanceCheck V2.2 — 18 Original Skill Map

**Accountant/finance workflows** (modeled on published capability descriptions, NOT vendor code):
1. `financial-claim-check` — structured public-filing claims
2. `accounting-close-review` — educational close arithmetic
3. `public-data-privacy-gate` — mandatory privacy/IP/licensing boundary
4. `valuation-model-review` — DCF, comps and simplified LBO assumptions
5. `sox-testing` — synthetic selections/workpaper templates
6. `audit-support` — SOX 404 and audit documentation
7. `reconciliation` — GL vs bank/subledger arithmetic
8. `journal-entry-prep` — accrual, depreciation, prepaid and revenue exercises
9. `variance-analysis` — driver residual/bridge education
10. `financial-statements` — P&L, balance-sheet and cash flow checks
11. `close-management` — fictional close dependencies and blockers
12. `statistical-analysis` — bounded descriptive measures, no invented significance
13. `validate-data` — duplicate/missing/schema checks
14. `sql-queries` — conservative read-only syntax *not execution*
15. `dashboards` — source-linked analytic display specifications
16. `capital-iq-kensho` — **licensed integration template only, no S&P access**
17. `connectors-governance` — Snowflake/Databricks/BigQuery/M365/Slack/ERP candidate approval rules; **disconnected**
18. `reviewer-engineer` — ten release gates: data, provenance, numerics, construct, bias, cyber, GDPR/AI Act/IP, reproducibility, human authority
