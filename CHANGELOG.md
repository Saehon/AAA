# Changelog

All material changes to **AAA — Audit & Accounting AI Laboratory** should be summarized here in addition to the Git history.

AAA is research software. Versioning and repository documentation do not imply audit-firm endorsement, regulator approval, professional certification, or production readiness.

## [Unreleased] — 2026-09-14

### Added
- Professional repository baseline aligned with the NAAIL Product Repository Standard.
- `ROADMAP.md` for staged research and engineering priorities.
- `SECURITY.md` for credentials, confidential data, model/API and disclosure controls.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` for research contributions and provenance expectations.
- `REPRODUCIBILITY.md` for experiment and notebook reproduction requirements.
- `DATA_SOURCES.md` for data provenance, rights, and evidence-class boundaries.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for software, model, standards, dataset, and trademark boundaries.

### Governance
- AI/model output is not treated as audit evidence by itself.
- Results from synthetic, proxy, or exploratory experiments must be labelled accordingly.
- Material professional conclusions require qualified human review.
- Third-party code, data, standards, publications, models, and trademarks retain their own rights.

## Earlier research material

The repository contains notebooks and experimental assets created before this professional baseline. Earlier material may not yet satisfy the current documentation, dependency-pinning, data-lineage, testing, or reproducibility standard. Such material should be treated as legacy research content until reviewed and migrated.
