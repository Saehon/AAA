# AAA — Audit & Accounting AI Laboratory Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Repository discipline
- classify legacy notebooks and prototypes;
- document data provenance, licenses, and research purpose;
- standardize study folders and run manifests;
- separate synthetic demonstrations from empirical evidence.

## Phase 2 — Reproducible audit/accounting studies
- add repeatable environments and study-level instructions;
- define frozen evaluation datasets where legally usable;
- record model versions, prompts, seeds, and configurations;
- preserve negative and failed experiments.

## Phase 3 — Benchmarking and scientific validation
- compare baseline ML/NLP/LLM methods;
- add temporal/out-of-sample testing;
- adversarial critic/replicator review;
- calibration, robustness, falsification, and sensitivity analysis;
- connect results to Evidence Passport / Chain-of-Evidence artifacts.

## Phase 4 — Professional-intelligence integration
- connect selected experiments to POMELO/VERA evaluation interfaces;
- support KIWI CAM/KAM and ICFR research modules;
- strengthen deterministic verification and human-review gates;
- add governed digital-twin experiments.

## Boundary

AAA is a research laboratory. Roadmap items do not imply audit-firm endorsement, regulatory approval, production readiness, legal compliance, or professional assurance.
