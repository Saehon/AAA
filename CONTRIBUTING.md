# Contributing to AAA — Audit & Accounting AI Laboratory

Contributions are welcome when they improve research quality, reproducibility, documentation, evaluation, or safety.

## Principles

1. Distinguish synthetic demonstrations from empirical findings.
2. Document data origin, transformations, exclusions, and rights.
3. Do not upload confidential client information or restricted standards material.
4. Record model/software versions and important configuration choices.
5. Add tests or reproducible notebooks for material changes where practical.
6. Do not treat LLM outputs or agent consensus as professional evidence.
7. Preserve material negative results, failed runs, and known limitations.
8. Require human review for professional accounting/audit conclusions.

## Pull requests

A useful pull request should explain the research/problem context, proposed change, evidence or rationale, reproduction steps, data/license implications, evaluation effect, and remaining limitations.

For empirical work, document the research question, sample, variables, method, evaluation metrics, robustness tests, and replication artifacts.

By contributing, you confirm that you have the right to submit the material under the repository's applicable terms.
